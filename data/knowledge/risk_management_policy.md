# Business Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines principles for using analytical and machine-learning signals in business risk management.

## Risk Signals

Risk predictions are analytical indicators rather than guaranteed outcomes.

Business decisions should consider:

- Model predictions.
- Customer transaction history.
- Product and revenue trends.
- Operational information.
- Relevant business policies.

## Risk Review

Medium- and high-risk cases should receive additional investigation before significant business actions are taken.

## Model Limitations

Machine-learning predictions can contain false positives and false negatives.

Model outputs should therefore be interpreted as decision-support information rather than absolute conclusions.

## Evidence Requirement

Business recommendations should be supported by observable data, model outputs, or documented business policies.

The system must not invent evidence or unsupported policy statements.
