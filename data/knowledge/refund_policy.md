# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the general handling principles for customer refunds and returned orders.

## Returned Orders

Returned orders should be reviewed to understand the underlying reason for the return.

A high return rate may indicate:

- Product quality concerns.
- Product expectation mismatch.
- Delivery or fulfillment issues.
- Customer-specific purchasing behavior.

## Refund Review

Refund-related cases should be investigated using available order and customer information.

Business teams should review:

- Order status.
- Product involved.
- Customer history.
- Frequency of returns.
- Related customer complaints.

## Escalation

Repeated or unusually high return activity should be investigated by the responsible business team.

Refund activity should not automatically be interpreted as customer churn. It should be considered together with other behavioral signals.
