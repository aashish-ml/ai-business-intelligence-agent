# Sales Escalation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when unusual sales or customer-performance patterns should receive additional business investigation.

## Revenue Decline

A significant decline in revenue should trigger investigation of possible contributing factors, including:

- Product performance.
- Customer segment performance.
- Order volume.
- Geographic performance.
- Discounts.
- Returns and cancellations.

## Product Performance

Products with declining revenue or weak estimated profitability should be reviewed for potential business causes.

## Customer Segments

Significant changes in customer-segment performance should be investigated before strategic actions are taken.

## Escalation

When an analytical result indicates a material business concern, the responsible business team should review the underlying evidence before making decisions.

## Evidence Requirement

Escalation decisions should be based on verified business data and relevant documented policies.
