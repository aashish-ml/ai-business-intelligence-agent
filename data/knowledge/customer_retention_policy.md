# Customer Retention Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard approach for identifying and responding to customers who may be at risk of churn.

## Risk Categories

Customers are classified into three operational risk categories based on the customer risk model:

- Low Risk: predicted risk probability below 35%.
- Medium Risk: predicted risk probability from 35% up to, but not including, 60%.
- High Risk: predicted risk probability of 60% or higher.

The risk classification is an analytical signal and should be combined with customer behavior and business context before taking action.

## Low-Risk Customers

Low-risk customers generally do not require immediate retention intervention.

Recommended actions include:

- Continue normal customer engagement.
- Monitor purchasing activity.
- Maintain standard service quality.
- Review changes in behavior during regular business monitoring.

## Medium-Risk Customers

Medium-risk customers should receive proactive monitoring.

Recommended actions include:

- Review recent purchasing behavior.
- Check for declining order frequency or spending.
- Review recent returns or cancellations.
- Consider targeted customer engagement.
- Escalate to the customer success team when additional investigation is required.

## High-Risk Customers

High-risk customers require priority review.

Recommended actions include:

- Review the customer's recent transaction history.
- Investigate significant changes in purchasing behavior.
- Review service, return, cancellation, or discount-related issues.
- Initiate appropriate retention outreach.
- Record the investigation and recommended action.

## Important Limitation

Risk predictions must not be treated as guaranteed churn outcomes. The model provides a probability-based risk signal and should support, not replace, business judgment.
