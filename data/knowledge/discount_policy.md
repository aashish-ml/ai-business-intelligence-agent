# Discount Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines principles for evaluating customer and product discount activity.

## Discount Monitoring

Discount usage should be monitored when analyzing:

- Revenue performance.
- Customer profitability.
- Product margins.
- Customer retention behavior.

## Business Impact

Discounts can increase customer engagement but may reduce realized revenue and profit margin.

Business analysis should therefore consider both:

- Gross transaction value.
- Discount-adjusted revenue and estimated profit.

## Investigation

Unusually high discount activity should be investigated when it materially affects product profitability or customer economics.

Discount percentage alone should not be used as the sole indicator of customer or product performance.
